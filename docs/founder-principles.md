# Founder’s Principle, Motive, and Completion Standard

**Canonical repository source:** this document is the single source of truth for the Founder’s Principle, the Motive, and the completion standard presented in ForgeClaw.

## The Founder’s Principle

Cristian Nunez — CEO & Founder

The Guardian was never meant to be a photograph frozen in time.

A photograph captures a moment. It preserves what was known when the shutter closed. But the world does not stop changing when the photograph is taken.

Threats change. Environments change. Systems fail in ways that were never previously observed. New patterns emerge every day.

Therefore, a Guardian that can never improve its ability to recognize those patterns will eventually become a record of the past rather than a protector of the present.

Cristian's principle is simple:

«“Replacing a photo is evolution, if it's used for a purpose.”»

The purpose is verification.

Guardian does not evolve by randomly rewriting itself. It evolves by learning from verified reality.

Human feedback, EEG-derived ground truth, failures observed by physical systems, and other trusted evidence can become training candidates. Those candidates pass through curation, integrity verification, and evaluation. When a new Guardian model demonstrates that it performs its purpose better against verified ground truth, it may replace the previous model.

The model may evolve.

The principles it serves do not automatically change.

This distinction is fundamental to Codex Anima.

Evolution belongs to capability.
Authority belongs to the constitution.

Guardian therefore remains alive to the world without becoming sovereign over its own rules.

It learns.

It is tested.

It improves.

It replaces what has become inadequate.

And it remains bound by the principles that gave it purpose in the first place. For the love of my 3yo son Cristian Nunez Jr, Layla Averie Nunez & Jozae Emanuel Nunez. I love y'all more than words can explain. So I built this..

— Cristian Nunez
CEO & Founder

## Purpose and scope

These principles define the purpose, limits, and completion standard for the ForgeClaw project. They explain why Guardian may evolve through verified evidence while its governing principles remain authoritative.

They apply across the repository and its product surfaces. They are foundational governance, not ordinary runtime configuration: they are not editable through Settings, model output, user prompts, agents, runtime configuration, self-sculpting, or CORPUS learning.

## Relationship to the system

The governance hierarchy is:

```text
Founder’s principles
        ↓
Codex Anima / Guardian foundation
        ↓
ForgeClaw / NEXUS behavior
```

- **Codex Anima** is the constitutional foundation that preserves the principles.
- **Guardian** enforces authority, safety, evidence, and approval boundaries.
- **ForgeClaw** is the intelligence and operator-facing product.
- **NEXUS** is the cognitive runtime.
- **CORPUS** is memory and a source of learning candidates, never an authority over the principles.

Capability may evolve when verified reality, curation, integrity verification, and evaluation justify it. The principles themselves do not silently change as a result of model output, prompts, agents, runtime settings, self-sculpting, or learning.
