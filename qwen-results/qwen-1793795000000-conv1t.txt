The phenomenon you're referring to, quantum entanglement, is attributed to the fundamental principles of quantum mechanics. It suggests a deeper interconnectedness in the universe that challenges classical notions of locality and separability. This phenomenon is not just a theoretical concept but has been experimentally verified through various studies and experiments, such as those conducted by Alain Aspect and others in the 1980s.
