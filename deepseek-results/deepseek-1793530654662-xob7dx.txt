Joshua Van won the UFC 331 flyweight title against Alexandre Pantoja by unanimous decision. Arman Tsarukyan defeated Mauricio Ruffy via first-round TKO. Sean Sharaf shocked Gable Steveson with a first-round knockout, marking the third-fastest KO in UFC heavyweight history. Natalia Silva won the UFC Women's Flyweight Championship against Wang Cong. Payton Talbott defeated Deiveson Figueiredo via first-round TKO, and Esteban Ribovics stopped King Green in the first round. Other notable finishes include Imanol Rodriguez defeating Alden Coria via KO, Daniel Pinas defeating Andrey Pulaev via KO, Marcus McGhee defeating Anthony Romero via TKO, Anthony Wint defeating Lucas Armand via TKO, Johnny Walker defeating Mick Parkin via KO, Jacobe Smith defeating Bruce Whitehead via KO, Alexander Hernandez defeating Rafael dos Anjos via TKO, and Marvin Vettori losing to Ismail Naurdiev via unanimous decision.
