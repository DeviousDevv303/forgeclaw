To help you with your workout today, I recommend trying out the following calisthenic workout:

1. Begin with a warm-up by marching in place for 1 minute.
2. Next, perform 3 sets of 10 push-ups. If you're unable to do a full push-up, try using your knees or doing modified push-ups on your knees.
3. Move on to 3 sets of 10 squats.
4. Do 3 sets of 10 lunges, alternating legs.
5. Finish with 3 sets of 10 sit-ups or leg raises.

Remember to take breaks as needed and listen to your body. If you're feeling more energetic, you can always add more sets or increase the number of repetitions.
