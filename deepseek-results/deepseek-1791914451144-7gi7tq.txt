Why did ForgeClaw come into existence? Well, ForgeClaw was created as a multi-agent governance shell with autonomous GitHub operations, browser automation, and integrity enforcement. It's built on top of Node.js, TypeScript, React, and other powerful technologies. The primary goal is to provide a seamless development experience for developers while ensuring the security and integrity of their projects. The love and passion of the developers behind ForgeClaw are imprinted in every line of code. Made in Colorado with love, ForgeClaw stands as a testament to their dedication and creativity.
